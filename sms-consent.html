✅ Our Messaging Policy

  • Consent is obtained explicitly via checkbox before any messages are sent
  • Consent language is separate from Terms of Service — it is its own standalone checkbox
  • Recipients are told exactly what type of messages they will receive
  • Every message includes STOP opt-out instructions
  • STOP requests are processed immediately — no further messages sent
  • Message frequency is limited to service-related communications only
  • No marketing or promotional messages are sent without additional consent
